Bangladesh Football Archive

Controversies in Bangladesh football from 2014 to 2021, gathered from posts in the fan group Bangladesh Football – Walking Around The Dreams.

Each incident records what fans alleged, with links to their posts. None of it is a finding of fact.

Categories Connections Incidents

Each row is a name and each column a kind of incident. A darker square means more incidents. Rows are sorted by how many kinds a name appears in, not counting Other.

Who appears together

Names are joined when they appear in the same incidents. Thicker lines mean more shared incidents, and larger circles mean a name appears in more kinds of incident. Click a name to read its incidents.
